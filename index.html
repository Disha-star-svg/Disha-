01 About Me

A foundation in science,
a goal in healthcare.

I am a science student from Dhaka with a keen interest in healthcare and the pharmaceutical sciences. Through my school studies, I have built a foundation in scientific thinking and learned to ask how classroom concepts connect with real-life health questions.

I enjoy learning how medicines are discovered, tested and used safely, and I am especially interested in research that can improve public health. Alongside my academic work, I value communication, teamwork and time management because clear thinking and collaboration matter in any healthcare-related path.

I am currently in my second year in the Department of Pharmacy at Manarat International University. University life has given me the chance to study healthcare and pharmaceutical topics more deeply and to keep growing as a learner.
